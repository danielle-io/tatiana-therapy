A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

A little clarity
before we begin.

It's okay to have questions.
There's space for those, too.

Is therapy available where I live?

Tatiana offers virtual therapy throughout California. Reach out to discuss whether her services are a fit for your location and needs.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

You don't need a diagnosis or a perfectly organized explanation to reach out. A free consultation is a chance to share what's on your mind, ask questions, and explore fit.

What approaches do you use?

Tatiana draws on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP), tailoring the work to your experiences and goals. You can ask how these approaches might apply to you during a consultation.

How do I find out about fees and insurance?

Contact Tatiana directly for current session fees, insurance information, and availability. This preview does not list or confirm rates, coverage, or open appointments.

How do I take the first step?

Use the phone or email links below to contact Tatiana at her existing practice. Keep initial messages brief and avoid including sensitive health information. This website preview does not collect or send any messages.
